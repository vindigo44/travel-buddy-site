Terms of use and subscription

Last updated: 2 November 2026

IN SHORT
  • Free: 1 trip with everything in it, and as many souvenir photos as you like.
  • Premium: €2.99 a month, €19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or €34.99 once, for life.
  • Apple or Google take the payment and handle refunds.
  • Cancel anytime. If Premium ends, nothing is deleted.

These terms cover Travel Buddy Premium, the optional subscription (or one-time lifetime purchase) in the Travel Buddy app. Your use of the app itself is governed by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement when you get it from the App Store, and by the Google Play Terms of Service when you get it from Google Play. Nothing in these terms limits the rights you have as a consumer under the law of the country you live in.

Free and Premium

  • Free: one trip with everything in it — budget, expenses in any currency, itinerary, checklist, widgets, exports and the account backup — and souvenir photos without limit.
  • Premium: as many trips as you like, sharing your trips with your travel companions (they join for free), and importing backups that hold several trips.
  • No account is needed, for the free version or for Premium.

Prices and billing

  • Monthly: €2.99 a month. Yearly: €19.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial if you haven't had one for Premium before. In other countries the store sets the price in your currency; the app shows the exact price, taxes included, before you confirm.
  • Lifetime: €34.99, paid once. It is not a subscription: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel, and Premium stays on your Apple ID or Google account for good.
  • Payment is made through your Apple ID or Google account: when you confirm the purchase, or at the end of the free trial.
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • If the price changes, the store tells you beforehand and, where the law or its rules require it, asks for your agreement.

Cancelling

Cancel at any time in the app (Profile → Premium → Manage my subscription), on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or on Android in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Premium then stays until the end of the period already paid, and nothing more is charged. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged. Deleting the app or your Travel Buddy account does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds and withdrawal

Apple and Google take the payment, so they handle refunds under their own rules: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android from your Google Play order history. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, your right of withdrawal from a purchase made at a distance is handled by the store you bought from, as described in Apple's Media Services Terms and the Google Play Terms of Service. If something went wrong, write to us as well.

When Premium ends

Nothing is deleted. Every trip and photo you already have stays: you can still view, edit, export and back them up. Only planning another trip needs Premium again.

On a new phone

Premium belongs to the Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with: tap Restore purchases in the Premium sheet. If you were signed in to a Travel Buddy account when you subscribed, Premium also follows that account to your other phones.

The app

  • Exchange rates are indicative market rates, given for convenience: they are not financial advice, and your bank or exchange office may apply other rates.
  • We work to keep the app and the account backup running, but we can't promise they will never be interrupted. Your trips are always kept on your phone first; exporting a backup now and then is a good habit.

Changes

If these terms change, the new version is published on this page with a new date. A change never applies to a period you have already paid for.

Contact

Travel Buddy is published by an independent developer. Questions about Premium or these terms: travelbuddystudio@gmail.com. How your data is handled: privacy policy.

Gratuit et Premium

  • Gratuit : un voyage avec tout ce qu'il contient — budget, dépenses dans toutes les devises, itinéraire, checklist, widgets, exports et sauvegarde dans le compte — et des photos souvenirs sans limite.

Prix et facturation

  • Mensuel : 2,99 € par mois. Annuel : 19,99 € par an, avec 7 jours d'essai gratuit si vous n'en avez pas déjà eu pour Premium. Dans les autres pays, le store fixe le prix dans votre devise ; l'app affiche le prix exact, taxes comprises, avant que vous confirmiez.

Résiliation

Résiliez à tout moment dans l'app (Profil → Premium → Gérer mon abonnement), sur iPhone dans Réglages → votre nom → Abonnements, ou sur Android dans Google Play → Profil → Paiements et abonnements → Abonnements. Premium reste alors actif jusqu'à la fin de la période déjà payée, et plus rien n'est facturé. Si vous résiliez pendant l'essai gratuit, vous n'êtes pas facturé. Supprimer l'app ou votre compte Travel Buddy ne résilie pas l'abonnement.

Apple et Google encaissent le paiement : ce sont donc eux qui gèrent les remboursements, selon leurs propres règles — sur iPhone sur reportaproblem.apple.com, sur Android depuis l'historique des commandes Google Play. En cas de problème, écrivez-nous aussi.

Quand Premium s'arrête

Rien n'est supprimé. Tous vos voyages et photos restent : vous pouvez toujours les consulter, les modifier, les exporter et les sauvegarder. Seule la préparation d'un autre voyage demande à nouveau Premium.

Sur un nouveau téléphone

Premium est lié à l'identifiant Apple ou au compte Google avec lequel vous vous êtes abonné : touchez Restaurer mes achats dans la fiche Premium. Si vous étiez connecté à un compte Travel Buddy en vous abonnant, Premium suit aussi ce compte sur vos autres téléphones.
